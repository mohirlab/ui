`Popover` and `Tooltip`, built on the native Popover API and CSS anchor positioning: no JavaScript positioning. Popovers light-dismiss, close on Escape and flip at viewport edges; tooltips show on hover (after a delay) and on focus, hide on Escape, and describe their trigger with `aria-describedby`. `placement` takes `top`, `bottom`, `start` and `end`, plus `-start`/`-end` alignments.
